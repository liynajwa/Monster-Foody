How to play

1. Move Mamee Monster with the arrows.
2. Tap a glowing food to collect it.
3. Find both foods for your mission.
4. Put the word cards in order.
5. Check your sentence!

100 points for a correct answer on your first try. 50 points if you fix it after a mistake. Try your best!
